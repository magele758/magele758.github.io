Privacy Policy — PageLens Agent

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

**Maintainer:** OPC Studio

**Contact:** support@example.com

1. Local-First Privacy Commitment

**PageLens Agent** (`pagelens-agent`) is built on a **Local-First, Privacy-by-Design** architecture: Open-Core 可审计代码 · 受控填表人工确认 · 网页正文零中转.

• 不设官方后端代理服务器，网页内容只从你的浏览器直发给你填写的模型端点或本机 Ollama。

• 所有表单写入均遵循受控预览机制，绝不自动触发转账、删除、外发邮件等不可逆动作。

• 开源核心透明可查，Pro 离线密钥基于 Ed25519 本地验签。

Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Direct Connection

• **PageLens Agent** supports BYOK architecture. Any API keys you enter (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or local Ollama endpoints) are stored **exclusively on your local device** (`chrome.storage.local` or OS keychain).

• Requests transmit directly from your client environment to your chosen AI provider. **OPC Studio** never proxies, logs, or retains your BYOK credentials or raw prompts.

Browser Extension Permissions Disclosure

We strictly adhere to the Chrome Web Store Principle of Least Privilege:

• **`activeTab`**: 仅在用户呼出侧栏并发起分析或填表指令时读取当前活动页面 DOM 摘要。

• **`storage`**: 本地存储用户自带模型 API Key、提取 Schema 模板与离线授权码。

• **`sidePanel`**: 提供常驻不遮挡正文的 Chrome 原生侧边栏工作台，用于结构化结果预览与填表差异对比。

• **`scripting`**: 在用户显式点击确认后，向当前活动页面表单控件注入受控字段值并高亮变更预览，永不自动点击最终支付或不可逆提交按钮。

2. Data We Collect (Minimalist Scope)

1. **Core Product Usage:** Zero raw page DOMs, private PDFs, audio streams, or Git source repositories are uploaded to OPC Studio.

2. **Waitlist & Optional Updates:** When you voluntarily join our waitlist, we store a SHA-256 cryptographic hash of your email address (and masked preview when `PRIVACY_MASK_EMAIL=1`) plus aggregate UTM channel attribution (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`).

3. **Offline Ed25519 License Verification:** Pro licenses are verified locally using an embedded Ed25519 public key signature without mandatory continuous phone-home tracking.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Brokers

We **never** sell, rent, or trade user data to advertisers, data brokers, or credit agencies. Data is never used to train foundation models.

4. Your Rights & Deletion Requests

You may clear all local state at any time by uninstalling the extension/application or clearing local storage. For waitlist removal or privacy inquiries, email **support@example.com**.
